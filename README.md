## What could this potentially look like at its completion?

Using a list of underdog adp, it has a list of players being drafted in the 2026 fantasy football drafts. It then pairs their name which should coincide with an ID, to that players information. What team they are on, their previous stats, projected stats, projected team stats
, etc. Based on that information it assigns that player a score, and then every player is ranked based on their score. It is then matched up to the current ADP on underdog and displays players that are a good or poor value at their underdog ADP. It may even suggest
team structures as well. 

A machine learning model trained on old fantasy football drafts, which ones were negative and succesful to determine strategies that are most or least viable (This actually could be done in line with the first project too, so we give every player a score, 
and the ML model determines the best draft strategies or trends in succesful drafts and we combine that to not just find player values but draft overall good teams)
